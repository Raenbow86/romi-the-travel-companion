# ROMI — The Travel Companion

A mobile-first road-life companion for nomads, campers, RVers, seasonal workers, and anyone trying to find the next honest stop.

Live: [https://romi-the-travel-companion.vercel.app/](https://romi-the-travel-companion.vercel.app/)

## Mission

Help people find what they actually need on the road — sleep, water, shower, food, fuel, laundry, dogs, adult-friendly, adventure — using real places and honest traveler reports. Trust is the product.

## Visual truth (keep forever)

- **Teal = scout verified.** Someone went. ROMI will stand behind it.
- **Orange = Google lead.** A listing, not a confirmation. Needs a scout.

Google is a lead to filter, never ROMI’s fact.

## What’s working (September 2026)

- Explore: where + chips + map + cards, any town or ZIP
- Gold list from real Gunnison / Almont / Paonia trips
- Scout form on the Scouts tab
- Google hours: confirm yes, or edit if wrong
- Traveler reviews on teal cards, newest first
- Needs-a-new-scout → Angela → scouts
- Reports can email off the phone (see `SCOUT-PIPELINE.md`)

## What’s not yet

- Accurate chip filtering (biggest gap)
- Scout logins
- ROMI email domain
- Founding-scout points / gifts in the app
- AI companion talking while you drive in
